The client synchronously cancels resume and delayed tickets at every explicit entry point, including a refused monitor choice. A handover ticket contains its original intent version, peer, host launch and revision. A delayed closed-window fallback stores one immutable ticket; later idle catalogs cannot replace its revision or bypass its delay. First-idle opportunities are consumed once. A new connection opportunity cannot revive a still pending or failed explicit choice, including None. A successful source, monitor or launch catalog with a changed revision and a new valid video epoch creates a new closed-window recovery opportunity. None and failed choices do not. A changed admission revision with the previous video epoch is not a success acknowledgment.
